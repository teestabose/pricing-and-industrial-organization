# Pricing and Industrial Organization

Applied projects on demand estimation, market power, and pricing, using structural and reduced-form methods in Python.

| Project | Question | Methods | Key result |
|---|---|---|---|
| [BLP Merger Simulation](blp-merger-simulation/) | How would a four-firm merger and a delivery-time cap change prices and consumer welfare? | Random-coefficients logit (BLP), GMM, Bertrand-Nash equilibrium, pyblp | Merger cuts consumer surplus by about 13% per market; delivery cap raises it by about 6% |

## Skills
Demand estimation, elasticities and diversion ratios, markup and Lerner index analysis, merger simulation, counterfactual policy analysis, HHI and market concentration

Tools: Python, pyblp, pandas, NumPy, Matplotlib
